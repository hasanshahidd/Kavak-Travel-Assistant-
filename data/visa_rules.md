# Visa Rules

> Last updated: 2026-04-01. Always verify with the destination embassy before travel.

## UAE passport - Japan

UAE passport holders can enter Japan visa-free for tourism for up to 30 days. The passport must be valid for at least 6 months from the date of arrival, and proof of onward travel may be requested at immigration.

## UAE passport - United Kingdom

UAE passport holders require an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) for visits up to 6 months. The ETA costs GBP 10, is valid for 2 years, and must be obtained before boarding. Passport validity must cover the duration of the stay.

## UAE passport - Schengen Area (France, Germany, Italy, etc.)

UAE passport holders can enter the Schengen Area visa-free for up to 90 days within any 180-day period for tourism or business. From mid-2025, ETIAS authorisation is required online before travel. Passport must be valid for 3 months beyond the planned departure date from Schengen.

## UAE passport - United States

UAE passport holders are NOT eligible for the Visa Waiver Program. A B-1/B-2 visitor visa is required for tourism or business and must be obtained at a US embassy or consulate before travel. Processing time is typically 2-4 weeks.

## UAE passport - Australia

UAE passport holders require an eVisitor or Visitor visa (subclass 600) before travel. The eVisitor (subclass 651) allows multiple stays of up to 3 months each within a 12-month period and is free of charge. Apply online via the Australian Department of Home Affairs.

## Indian passport - Japan

Indian passport holders require a tourist visa for Japan. The single-entry tourist visa allows stays of up to 90 days and must be obtained from a Japanese embassy or consulate before travel. Recent approvals also include an eVisa option for tourists.

## Indian passport - United Kingdom

Indian passport holders require a Standard Visitor visa for tourism, valid for up to 6 months. Application is online and processing takes around 3 weeks. Passport must be valid for the entire duration of the stay.

## UK passport - Japan

UK passport holders can enter Japan visa-free for up to 90 days for tourism. Passport must be valid for the duration of the stay.

## UK passport - United States

UK passport holders are eligible for the Visa Waiver Program via ESTA. ESTA must be obtained online before travel, costs USD 21, and is valid for 2 years or until the passport expires.

## Indian passport - Schengen Area (France, Germany, Italy, etc.)

Indian passport holders require a Schengen Short-Stay (Type C) visa for tourism or business stays of up to 90 days within any 180-day period. Apply at the consulate of the primary destination country at least 15 days before travel. Required documents include a flight reservation, accommodation proof, and travel insurance covering at least EUR 30,000.

## Indian passport - United States

Indian passport holders require a B-1 (business) or B-2 (tourism) visitor visa, obtained from a US embassy or consulate. Visa interviews are usually mandatory; processing time is typically 2-6 months due to high demand. The visa is generally issued for 10 years with multiple entries.

## Indian passport - Australia

Indian passport holders require a Visitor visa (subclass 600) or an Electronic Travel Authority. The Visitor visa allows stays of up to 3, 6, or 12 months depending on the stream. Apply online via the Australian Department of Home Affairs at least 4 weeks before travel.

## UK passport - Schengen Area (France, Germany, Italy, etc.)

UK passport holders can enter the Schengen Area visa-free for up to 90 days within any 180-day period for tourism or business. From mid-2025, ETIAS authorisation is required online before travel. Passport must be valid for 3 months beyond the planned departure date from Schengen and issued within the last 10 years.

## UK passport - Australia

UK passport holders require an eVisitor (subclass 651) before travel. The eVisitor is free, processed online, and allows multiple stays of up to 3 months each within a 12-month period.

## Pakistani passport - Japan

Pakistani passport holders require a tourist visa for Japan. The single-entry tourist visa allows stays of up to 90 days and must be obtained from a Japanese embassy or consulate before travel. Required documents include a flight itinerary, accommodation booking, and bank statements covering at least 3 months.

## Pakistani passport - United Kingdom

Pakistani passport holders require a Standard Visitor visa for tourism, valid for up to 6 months. Application is online and processing typically takes 3-6 weeks. Required documents include proof of accommodation, return flight booking, and financial evidence.

## Pakistani passport - Schengen Area (France, Germany, Italy, etc.)

Pakistani passport holders require a Schengen Short-Stay (Type C) visa for tourism or business stays of up to 90 days within any 180-day period. Processing typically takes 15-45 days. Travel insurance covering at least EUR 30,000 is mandatory.

## Pakistani passport - United States

Pakistani passport holders require a B-1 or B-2 visitor visa from a US embassy or consulate. Visa interviews are mandatory; processing times can range from 2 to 12 months. Administrative processing may add additional delays.

## Saudi passport - Schengen Area (France, Germany, Italy, etc.)

Saudi passport holders can enter the Schengen Area visa-free for up to 90 days within any 180-day period for tourism. From mid-2025, ETIAS authorisation is required online before travel. Passport must be valid for 3 months beyond the planned departure date.

## Saudi passport - United Kingdom

Saudi passport holders are eligible for the UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) for visits up to 6 months. The ETA costs GBP 10 and must be obtained before boarding.

## Egyptian passport - Schengen Area (France, Germany, Italy, etc.)

Egyptian passport holders require a Schengen Short-Stay (Type C) visa. Stays are limited to 90 days within any 180-day period. Application requires confirmed flight bookings, hotel reservations, and travel insurance covering at least EUR 30,000.

## Filipino passport - Japan

Filipino passport holders require a tourist visa for Japan. Multiple-entry visas are available for those with prior Japan travel history. Single-entry visas are processed in 5-7 working days when applied through accredited travel agencies.

## General requirements

For all destinations, travellers should ensure passports have at least 6 months of validity beyond the planned return date and at least two blank pages. Children require their own passports and may require additional consent documentation if travelling with one parent or unaccompanied.
